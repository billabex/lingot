feat(ui-tokens): add spacing.6xl = 80px

Extends the primitive spacing scale with an 80px step — useful for
hero pages, welcome / empty-state layouts, and top padding on
centered single-column pages (e.g. the Onboarding View template).

Follows the existing scale: xs 4, sm 6, md 8, lg 12, xl 16, 2xl 24,
3xl 32, 4xl 48, 5xl 64, **6xl 80**.

Exposed via the `billabexPreset` under both `spacing` and `sizes`.
